## 6.0 - Strait Bible

The program is now called **Strait Bible** and has a logo of its own, a gate with a scroll behind it. It is based on
Unbound Bible 5.4 by Vladimir Rybant; this is the first version of the new project. Your Bibles, notes and settings from
Unbound Bible are found and used as they are.

**Look**
- Themes: light, dark, sepia, solarized dark, high contrast and your own (editor included); a night theme that switches on
  by itself from 19:00 to 07:00
- On Windows the whole window follows the theme: title bar, menu bar, drop-down menus, tabs, scroll bars, lists, status
  bar, dialogs and the colors of the text
- Your own fonts (Add font), background pictures (cover, stretch, tile, with a visibility slider), text margins,
  justified text, line spacing

**Reading**
- Bookmarks, five highlight colors, your own notes on verses, lists of all of them
- Reading plans: the Bible in a year, the New Testament in 90 days, Psalms and Proverbs in 60 days
- Verse of the day (also on startup), random verse, read aloud (Windows), chapter keys (Ctrl+Left/Right), zoom with
  Ctrl + mouse wheel, full screen (F11), Ctrl+G to go to a verse
- Grey notes of translations such as the Amplified Bible: shown, hidden, or shown when the pointer rests on them
- Compare page: next and previous verse links

**Copying and data**
- Copy Verses: translation name, abbreviated translation name
- Copy chapter, save chapter as text, back up and restore bookmarks, highlights, notes and reading plan
- Settings are saved as soon as they are changed

**Project**
- New name, logo, About box and links; credit to the original author kept; GPL v3
- Files for packaging on Linux (`packaging/`), an optional GitHub build for Linux
